Vehicle Size Guide

Small to Large Vans

  • Small Van: 2.3 m³ | ~500–700 kg payload
  • Medium Van: 5 m³ | ~1000–1200 kg payload
  • Large Van: 11–13 m³ | ~1200–1500 kg payload
  • Luton Van: 17–20 m³ | ~800–1000 kg payload

7.5 Tonne Truck

  • Volume: 30–35 m³
  • Payload: 2500–3500 kg

18 Tonne & 26 Tonne

  • 18T Volume: 40–45 m³ | 9000 kg payload
  • 26T Volume: 50–60 m³ | 16000 kg payload

Articulated & Mega Trailers

  • Artic: 80–100 m³ | Up to 26000 kg
  • Mega Trailer: 100–110 m³ | Taller load space
